The Colonial Exchange
Your goal: be a merchant in one of the thirteen colonies from 1763 until the war begins in 1775, and finish with the biggest fortune and a good name. Each year you decide what goods to carry and how to get them. Customers buy on their own, and Patriots and Loyalists want different things.
📦Load Your ShipmentEach year you can carry only so many goods. Pick which ones.
⏱️Watch the MarketSales happen by themselves while the year runs. Popularity shifts.
🕯️Pick Your RouteTaxed goods can be sold legally or smuggled in. Smuggling is risky.
⚖️Choose SidesBig choices stop the clock. Anger a faction and it may come for you.
