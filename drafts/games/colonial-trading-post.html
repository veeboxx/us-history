Quarter 1 · Founding a Nation

The Colonial Trading Post

It's 1750-something in a bustling colonial port. You run the trading post. Buy furs, lumber, fish, and grain from local suppliers, then decide where to sell — the legal English ship, or a faster-paying Dutch smuggler who technically isn't supposed to be here at all. Stock English cloth, tools, and tea to resell to the townsfolk. Survive 8 turns and build the most respected (or most feared) trading post in the colonies.

🧭

Two kinds of goods

Exports (furs, lumber, fish, grain) you buy locally and sell to ships. Imports (cloth, tools, tea) you buy from ships and resell to townsfolk.

⚖️

The Navigation Acts

Officially, exports are supposed to go to England first. The English ship pays fair, legal prices. A Dutch ship might show up and pay much better — if you're willing to risk it.

🕵️

Smuggling has a cost

Every trade with the Dutch ship raises your odds of a customs inspection. Get away with it and the town treats you like a folk hero. Get caught, and you'll pay for it.

📦

Limited warehouse

You can only hold so much stock at once, and each supplier only has so much to trade each turn. Plan ahead.
